# Sources for platforms.csv

**Key to the CSV columns**

- **`status`**
  - `reported` means the platform's owner disclosed the figure: a filing, an earnings release or call, an official blog post, or an executive's public statement. Where a company statement is cited through a press report, the press report is listed below.
  - `secondary` means a third-party measurement (comScore) or a figure whose basis the company never stated.
  - No row is `estimate`. Nothing has been interpolated.
- **`metric`**
  - `other` covers comScore monthly unique visitors, Tencent's early "active user accounts", Tumblr unique visitors, YouTube's 2006 comScore uniques, Telegram's 2014–2015 "active users", and region-limited figures. The source entry says which.
- **Year rule.** Each year uses the figure dated closest to 31 December. A figure published up to about six weeks after year end counts for the year before, for example Q4 earnings or a January milestone. Each figure is used once only. Where the date is far from year end, the source entry gives the "as of" date.
- **`owner`** is the owner when the figure was measured.

## Caveats

**Metric breaks inside a platform's series.** These breaks show up as a false jump or drop if the series is plotted as one line.

- **Twitter/X:**
  - 2010–2018 is MAU.
  - 2019–2022 is mDAU. For 2018, Twitter reported 321M MAU and also 126M mDAU.
  - 2022 is Q2, not Q4: the last filing before the take-private.
  - 2023–2025 are X's unaudited claims. They are on a different basis again ("monetizable" MAU), and third parties such as Similarweb dispute them.
  - **Twitter restatements:**
    - 2015 uses the restated 305M, which excludes SMS Fast Followers to match 2016 onward (320M as first reported).
    - 2020 and 2021 use the recast mDAU (190.9M, 214.7M). Twitter disclosed in 2022 that mDAU had been overstated from Q1 2019.
    - 2019 (152M) was never recast and is overstated by an undisclosed amount.
    - Twitter also revised Q4 2014 to 292M; the CSV keeps the 288M as first reported.
- **QQ:**
  - 2004–2011 is "active IM user accounts", which Tencent does not define.
  - 2012–2018 is total MAU, PC plus mobile.
  - 2019–2025 is mobile ("smart device") MAU only. The 807→647 step from 2018 to 2019 is a change of definition, not a collapse.
  - A consistent mobile series runs from 2013: 426, 576, 642, 652, 683.0, 699.8, then 647.0 for 2019, from sources 63–69.
- **Qzone:** 2009–2011 is "active user accounts". 2018 is smart-device MAU only (total MAU for 2017 was 563M; smart-device was 554M). Tencent stopped reporting Qzone after Q3 2019 (517M smart-device MAU, not in the CSV).
- **Reddit:**
  - 2018–2019 is MAU from company statements.
  - 2020 is plain DAU.
  - 2021 onward is DAUq, Reddit's logged-in-plus-logged-out daily metric.
  - The fall from 430 to 52 is the metric change. For an MAU-like line, the weekly WAUq series is in sources 117–119.
- **Snapchat:**
  - Snapchat is in DAU throughout, because Snap only began giving MAU in 2023. That understates it next to MAU platforms.
  - Snap's MAU figures are over 800M (Q4 2023) and 946M (Q4 2025). No Q4 2024 MAU was found.
  - DAU for 2014–2015 was cut by 4.8% to match Snap's later method.
- **Instagram:** 2010–2011 are registered users and 2012 onward is MAU. The 2012 value is the first MAU disclosure, made on 2013-01-17.
- **Telegram:** 2014–2015 is "active users" with no period stated. 2016 onward is MAU.
- **Douyin:** All values are DAU. The 2018 report also gave over 500M MAU. The 2020 figure (600M) includes Douyin Huoshan. There has been no official DAU since August 2020.
- **Google+:**
  - 2011 is sign-ups.
  - 2012–2013 is Google's "active in the stream" monthly figure.
  - Google's definition was generous: for example, interactions with the Google+ notification bell across Google properties counted. Treat these as upper bounds.
- **YouTube:** 2006 is comScore worldwide unique visitors aged 15+. 2013 is "unique users", which includes logged-out viewers. 2017–2019 is logged-in monthly users. No figure after "2 billion+" (2019) was found; YouTube now says only "billions".
- **MySpace, Friendster, Hi5, Orkut:**
  - These are comScore worldwide monthly unique visitors aged 15+. That is not company MAU, and it is measured mostly in June or November, not December.
  - Exceptions:
    - MySpace 2009 is **US only** (68.4M, News Corp), so it shows a false dip between 125 (2008) and 101 (2010).
    - Orkut 2010–2011 is **Brazil only**.
    - Friendster 2009 is registered accounts.
  - Consider dropping these rows from the race or labelling them.
- **Registered vs active:**
  - LinkedIn is registered members throughout, and LinkedIn says this overstates actual users. LinkedIn has never disclosed MAU.
  - Bluesky is registered accounts. Bluesky's 2025 report mentions MAU only as a ratio.
  - Mastodon is monthly active accounts. Mastodon's registered accounts were about 5.8M (2022) to 9.6M (2025).

**Reporting stopped or is sparse.**

- **Facebook:**
  - Meta stopped reporting Facebook MAU/DAU after Q4 2023, when it moved to "Family daily active people": 3.07B Facebook MAU at end 2023, the last figure.
  - On the Q4 2025 call Zuckerberg said only "more than 2 billion daily actives each on Facebook and WhatsApp". That is a DAU floor, so it is not in the CSV.
- **Instagram and WhatsApp:** These are milestone announcements, not regular reports. Years with no announcement are blank: Instagram 2019–2021 and 2023–2024; WhatsApp 2018 and 2020–2024. Meta's repeated "more than 2 billion" WhatsApp phrasing is a floor and was not used.
- **Messenger:** No standalone figure after 1.3B (Sep 2017).
- **TikTok:**
  - The 2018–2020 values come from TikTok's own sworn complaint; 2020 is as of July.
  - There has been no official global MAU since "over 1 billion" (Sep 2021).
  - Figures circulating for 2023–2025 (1.5B–1.6B+) come from ad-reach tools such as DataReportal, not from TikTok, so they were left out.
- **Threads:** 2025 is 400M as of Aug 2025. The Q4 2025 call gave no Threads MAU.
- **Tumblr:**
  - Tumblr mostly reported blog counts (30M blogs 2011; 100M+ 2013; 475M 2019). Blogs are not people, so those counts are **excluded** from the CSV.
  - 2013 is monthly unique visitors (Yahoo).
  - 2023 (11.5M MAU) has no stated basis.
- **Friendster, Hi5, Orkut:** Nothing citable was found before 2006 (comScore World Metrix starts there) or after 2008–2011. MySpace has no citable figure for 2004–2005.
- **WeChat:** No year-end MAU before 2013. Tencent gave only a registered figure (300M, March 2013).

**Conflicts and how they were resolved.**

- **TikTok Dec 2019:** Some outlets print 508M. The complaint figure, 507,552,660, is used.
- **Mastodon:** The widely quoted 2.5M was Rochko's November 2022 peak. The year-end API value (1.82M) is used instead, under the year rule.
- **Instagram 2011:** "15 million" (Jan 2012, Entrepreneur) conflicts with about 10M at end 2011 in other accounts. The dated 10M (Sep 2011) is used.
- **Messenger 800M:** Fox Business dates it Dec 2015; Adweek and IBTimes date it Jan 2016. Either way it stays in 2015.
- **YouTube 2018:** One outlet later says 1.9B in summer 2018. The company's May 2018 figure (1.8B) is used.
- **Twitter 2015:** 320M (first reported) vs 305M (restated). 305M is used; see above.
- **LinkedIn:** No member count was found for Dec 2016–2019 or for 2024–2025, because Microsoft 10-Ks for FY2017–19 and FY2024–25 give none.

- X 2023–2025, all unaudited claims.
- Tumblr 2023.
- Reddit 2018, where the exact 2018 date is unknown.
- Pinterest 2015 and Reddit 2020, which are press reports of company statements.
- Douyin 2018–2020, which are media reports of company data reports.
- Tencent FY2018 (source 68), which is a PR Newswire copy, not tencent.com.
