# Where is the understanding? (chinese-room)

An explainer of the Chinese Room and the Systems Reply, framed around whether language models
understand. Cut: `chinese-room/main`, style `chalk`, draft voice `am_michael`, score `coasys-ambient`.

## State

- 2026-11-07: script at its second draft (`scripts/2026-11-07-systems-reply.md`). Look chosen,
  storyboard and core-piece variants reviewed (James picked the originals: network brain, stick
  man, cutaway room). `styles/chalk` built. All eight scenes animated as a first full draft;
  voiced (6:17) and scored. Waiting on James's first full watch.

## Decisions

- Look: "night chalk". Near-black ground, chalk-white lines, Inter (light captions, bold spaced
  caps labels). Meaning is a glow in an aurora gradient (#5ef2b0 → #4fc3f7 → #b18cff) tied to
  each system, not to the screen, so a part shows one hue and only the whole shows the spectrum.
- Core pieces: network brain, stick-figure man, cutaway room (variant A of each).
- Understanding can be held in structure (a book, weights, connections), separately from whether
  it's experienced. James's position; the film argues it rather than the classic "only the running
  system understands" phrasing.
- No "if it's anywhere" hedge about human understanding: it's a given.
- Brain-region names appear as on-screen labels only; narration stays plain.
- Glyphs are abstract shapes, never real Chinese characters.

## Known rough edges (first draft)

- Scene 4's wide library glow reads as a tinted wash at the widest zoom (the shelf pattern is
  sub-pixel there); may want a coarser pattern at distance.
- Scene 4's numbers (40,000+ volumes, thousands of years per word) are back-of-envelope; source
  or soften before the final voice.
- The score is coasys-ambient; this film may want its own, quieter score.
- Scene 6's books streaming into the head are small at the end of their flight.
