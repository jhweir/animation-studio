# coasys-ambient: reference

The sound of Data's Coasys films: soft pads, a felt piano, bowed strings, plucks and bells under a
slow reverb. Calm and unobtrusive; it sits well under narration.

## Moods

| mood | sound | use for |
|---|---|---|
| `shade` | soft suspended pads (Gmaj9, Em9, Cmaj9, Gmaj7), darker filter | a calm opening |
| `dark` | low bowed strings (Asus2, Dm add9) | the problem, what's broken |
| `lift` | one bright D(add9) that swells in fast | the moment something rises |
| `warm` | warm moving pads through eleven chords | ours: the alternative, the solution |
| `resolve` | Dmaj9 turning to A(add9) on each line's last part | the close |

After the last word, Dmaj9 (add6) holds to the end.

## Layers

| layer | sound | typical place |
|---|---|---|
| `piano` | a low, sparse felt piano through the chords; stops before the span's last word fades | the opener |
| `heartbeat` | soft low plucks on the root at 100 bpm | under `dark` |
| `strings` | warm bowed strings that swell in and stay | the last scenes |
| `arpeggio` | a thin, steady pluck arpeggio | the middle, once the story moves |

## Cue sounds

| kind | sound |
|---|---|
| `hit` | big words landing (set by `beat`): a low piano note in `shade`, a soft knock in `dark`, a pluck elsewhere |
| `pop` | a short pluck, random pan |
| `seed` | two bells |
| `grow`, `braid`, `shimmer`, `ripple`, `warm` | shimmers at different heights |
| `lift` | a rising piano figure, then bells |
| `final` | a spread chord of bells (the end card) |
| `crack` | a scatter of bright noise: something breaking |
| `lock` | two clicks: a padlock |
| `thud` | a low knock |
| `mesh` | a fast sweep of high plucks: many things connecting |
| `drop`, `land`, `slide` | single plucks and bells |
| `keep` | two bells |
| `tap` | a wooden tap; `i` steps through three notes and across the stereo field |
| `tick` | a small click; `i` moves it across the stereo field |
| `one` | a low piano octave and a bell: a single important moment |
| `step` | two soft footsteps |

`caption` is deliberately silent.
